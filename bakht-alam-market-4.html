Our Story

Where Wana Comes Together

Bakht Alam Market stands as one of the most recognised trading points along the Jandola–Sarwakai–Wana Road, serving as the commercial backbone of Wana — the administrative centre of South Waziristan.

From farmers and craftsmen to merchants and travellers, the market welcomes all who come to trade, connect, and share in the rhythms of daily life in the region.
